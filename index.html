One. Concept and Management

Cost versus Expense

  • Functions: Planning, Directing, and Controlling.
  • Cost (Asset): Future benefit (capitalised).
  • Expense: Expired cost for current revenue.
Asset → Consumed → Expense
Two. Classification

Product versus Period

  • Product Cost: Factory items (Direct Materials, Direct Labour, Manufacturing Overhead) → Cost of Goods Manufactured.
  • Period Cost: Non-Manufacturing (Selling and Administration) → Statement of Profit or Loss.
Manufacturing Overhead = Indirect Factory + Indirect Materials
Three. Key Formulas

Prime and Conversion

  • Prime Cost: Direct Materials plus Direct Labour.
  • Conversion Cost: Direct Labour plus Manufacturing Overhead.
  • Fixed Cost: Total stays constant; per unit drops as output rises.
Total Product Cost = Direct Materials + Direct Labour + Manufacturing Overhead
